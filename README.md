# Faculty Research & Publication Database

## Project Description
The Faculty Research & Publication Database is a database management system designed to organize and manage faculty information, research activities, academic publications, research topics, and co-authorship relationships. It helps users store, access, and monitor research-related information in one organized system.

## Objectives
- To maintain organized records of faculty members.
- To record and manage academic research and publications.
- To track research topics and publication details.
- To manage relationships between faculty members and their publications.
- To make research information easier to access, update, and monitor.

## Key Features
- **Faculty Management:** Store faculty names, departments, and other relevant information.
- **Publication Management:** Record publication titles, publication dates, and other publication details.
- **Research Topic Management:** Organize research according to its topic or field.
- **Co-Authorship Tracking:** Identify faculty members who collaborate on publications.
- **Data Organization:** Keep research and publication records organized for easier retrieval.

## Technologies Used
- **AppSheet** – Application interface and database management.
- **Google Sheets** – Data storage and management.
- **GitHub** – Project documentation and information repository.

## Intended Users
This system is intended for faculty members, researchers, and academic administrators who need to organize and monitor research and publication records.

## Benefits
The system improves the organization of academic research information, supports collaboration tracking, reduces difficulties in retrieving records, and helps academic institutions manage faculty research activities more efficiently.

## How to Access
The project documentation is available through this GitHub repository. The AppSheet application can be accessed through the application link provided by the project owner.

## Conclusion
The Faculty Research & Publication Database provides an organized way to manage faculty research, academic publications, research topics, and co-authorship relationships. It supports efficient record management and makes research information easier to maintain and access.
